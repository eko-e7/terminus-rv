How this update comes together

A smart computer helper, called an AI assistant, does the filing. We do the living, and we decide what it gets to see.

  1. 1

    The assistant sorts it

Deployment model

Two-unit design. The trailer is headquarters. A forward vehicle heads out on its own to scout, resupply or work off-grid. The comms gear isn't bolted to either one, so it rides with whichever unit is moving. Our current forward vehicle can't tow, so HQ stays put for now, but the team can still deploy.
