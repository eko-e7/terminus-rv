    How this update comes together

    A smart computer helper, called an AI assistant, does the filing. We do the living, and we decide what it gets to see.

    1. 1
      We gather it
      Photos of labels and receipts, our emails, and the makers' manuals.
    2. 2
      We clean it first
      Names, numbers, and other private details come out before the assistant sees anything.
    3. 3
      The assistant sorts it
      It matches each manual to the exact model on our rig and files every fact with a note of where it came from. If anything looks private, it stops and asks us first.
    4. 4
      This page updates
      Only the safe, big-picture parts show up here for you.
